## Intelligent Driving page

### Safety language requirement

Always describe capability precisely. Do not imply unsupervised autonomy unless the feature is legally approved and actually offered in that market.

### Modules

- sensor suite.
- LiDAR where fitted.
- radar/cameras/ultrasonics.
- 360 awareness.
- lane centring.
- adaptive cruise.
- blind-spot monitoring.
- surround camera.
- parking intelligence.
- remote parking.
- driver monitoring.

### Visualization

Use blue overlays as functional ADAS visualization, consistent with the reference page, while ensuring critical information is also available as text.

## Technology page SEO

Use structured headings with descriptive topic terms. Avoid making hero slogans the only H1 content if they do not describe the topic.
