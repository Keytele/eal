# Legal Asset Placeholder Manifest

Legal pages should be text-first and accessible. Add visual assets only when an approved legal/compliance owner has established their purpose and accessibility treatment.

| Slot | Suggested filename | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessibility statement support art | legal-accessibility-support | Optional, decorative by default |
| Privacy/security support art | legal-privacy-support | Optional, decorative by default |
| Cookie preferences support art | legal-cookie-support | Optional, decorative by default |

Do not embed the actual legal terms, consent notices, price disclaimers, finance disclosures, or policy versions in images.

Phases 51–52 remain text-first. Any optional support art is decorative and must not be used as evidence of legal approval, security, privacy, or compliance.
